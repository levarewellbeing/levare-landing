How was my diet today?
R
You've logged 1,840 kcal today — 631 short of your 2,471 kcal target. Protein is tracking well at 178g against your 238g goal, so you're about 60g short there. Given your training session this morning, I'd suggest a high-protein evening meal. Your glucose has been trending borderline recently — a lower-carb dinner tonight would be a good call.
What's my biggest health risk right now?
R
Your fasting glucose of 6.1 mmol/L is borderline pre-diabetic. Combined with your average sleep of 6.2 hours and elevated CRP of 5.8 mg/L, there's a clear pattern — poor sleep drives cortisol, which raises glucose and inflammation. Prioritising sleep above 7 hours would likely move all three markers in the right direction within 8-12 weeks.
